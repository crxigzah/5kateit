How it works

Three steps, then it does the typing.

1

Open the app you're ordering from

5kateit opens Deliveroo, Just Eat or Uber Eats in your own Edge or Chrome. Log in once and it stays logged in.

2

Find codes

It searches HotUKDeals, Latest Deals and Reddit, picks out anything that looks like a code and ranks them by how many places mention them. Tell it if you're a Deliveroo Plus, Just Eat+ or Uber One member and it hunts for member-only deals too.

3

Test at checkout

With your basket at checkout, it tries each code in the promo box, reads what the site says, and re-applies the best one at the end.

FAQ

Good to know

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That's SmartScreen. It shows for any new app that isn't code-signed. Click More info then Run anyway. The full source is on GitHub if you'd rather check it first.

Will it get my account banned?

It tests at most 15 codes per run, types at human speed and waits a few seconds between tries. But automated use is against all three apps' terms, so there's always some risk. Use it on your own account at your own risk.

Why do so many codes fail?

Most codes are one-off, tied to certain accounts, or new-customer only. That's the point: 5kateit finds out in seconds instead of you trying them one by one.

It can't find the promo box

Sites change their checkout pages. The settings file shown at the bottom of the app lets you point it at the exact box and button, and updates will fix the defaults.

I pay for Deliveroo Plus, Just Eat+ or Uber One

Switch on the membership toggle for that app. 5kateit then also searches for member deals and tries those first. If you're not a member, member-only codes are skipped so they don't waste your tries.

Does it see my card details?

No. It only reads the page text to spot the result and the total, and never clicks pay. Your logins, codes and history stay on your PC.

Does it work on Mac?

Not as a download yet. It's a Python app, so it runs on Mac or Linux from source with python -m tcf.

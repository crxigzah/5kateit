How it works

Three taps. It does the typing.

1

Shop like normal

Fill your basket on Deliveroo, Just Eat or Uber Eats in your own browser and head to checkout. No new logins, nothing to switch to.

2

Open Snackhack and find codes

Click the Snackhack icon and it opens beside the page. It searches HotUKDeals, Latest Deals and Reddit and ranks what it finds.

3

Hit Try codes

Each code goes into the promo box. Snackhack reads the result, checks the new total, and leaves the best one applied.

Memberships

Got Plus, Just Eat+ or Uber One? Say so.

Flip the switch in the app and Snackhack hunts for member-only deals too and tries them first. Not a member? It skips those codes so they don't waste your tries. Same for new-customer codes.

Install

Add it in about a minute.

Download Snackhack
  1. Unzip it

    Right-click Snackhack-extension.zip and choose Extract All. Keep the folder somewhere it won't get deleted, like Documents.

  2. Open extensions

    Go to chrome://extensions in Chrome, Brave or Opera, or edge://extensions in Edge.

  3. Load it

    Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the Snackhack folder.

  4. Pin it

    Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Snackhack so it's one click away at checkout.

Snackhack is on its way to the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons. Once it's listed, installing is a single click and updates happen automatically.

FAQ

Good to know

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Opera GX and Vivaldi. Anything built on Chromium. In Opera it opens as a popup rather than a side panel. Firefox and Safari aren't supported yet.

Could it get my account banned?

It tries at most 15 codes per run, types at human speed and pauses between tries. Automated use is still against the apps' terms, so there's always some risk. Use it on your own account at your own risk.

Why do so many codes fail?

Most codes are one-offs, tied to certain accounts, or for new customers only. That's the point: Snackhack finds out in seconds instead of you trying them one by one.

It can't find the promo box

Make sure you're on the checkout page. Checkout pages change now and then, so if it still can't find it, open an issue on GitHub with what the Activity log says and it'll get fixed in an update.

What can it see?

It only runs on Deliveroo, Just Eat and Uber Eats pages, and only when you press a button. It reads the page text to spot the result and the total, and it never presses pay. Your codes and settings stay in your browser. Privacy.

Why does it need Developer mode?

Only until Snackhack is in the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons. Browsers ask for it when you install an extension from a file. After that it's a normal one-click install.

Snackhack

Privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026 (version 3.1)

Snackhack doesn't collect, sell or send your personal data anywhere. There's no account and no analytics.

What stays in your browser

  • The promo codes it finds or you paste in, whether each one worked and how much it saved.
  • A history of your checks (which codes were tried, which worked, the saving), used for your savings totals.
  • Your settings, such as whether you have Deliveroo Plus, Just Eat+ or Uber One.
  • A short activity log.

These are kept in your browser's extension storage on your device. Removing the extension deletes them.

Pages it reads

Snackhack only works on Deliveroo, Just Eat and Uber Eats. On those sites a small script checks whether the promo code box is showing, so the Try codes button can unlock; it doesn't send anything anywhere. Codes are only typed in when you press a button. It reads the checkout page's text to find the promo box, see whether a code worked and read the total. It never reads or fills in payment details and never places an order.

Sites it contacts

When you press Find codes or Try codes, Snackhack loads public pages from these sites to look for codes: HotUKDeals, Latest Deals, Reddit, VoucherCodes, MyVoucherCodes, Groupon, CouponFollow, Wethrift, Discoup, Savoo and DealHack. Nothing about you is sent to them beyond a normal page request, and no cookies are sent.

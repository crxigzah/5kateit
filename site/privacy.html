Snackhack

Privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Snackhack doesn't collect, sell or send your personal data anywhere. There's no account and no analytics.

What stays in your browser

  • The promo codes it finds or you paste in, and whether each one worked.
  • Your settings, such as whether you have Deliveroo Plus, Just Eat+ or Uber One.
  • A short activity log.

These are kept in your browser's extension storage on your device. Removing the extension deletes them.

Pages it reads

Snackhack only works on Deliveroo, Just Eat and Uber Eats, and only when you press a button in it. It reads the checkout page's text to find the promo box, see whether a code worked and read the total. It never reads or fills in payment details and never places an order.

Sites it contacts

When you press Find codes, Snackhack loads public search pages from HotUKDeals, Latest Deals and Reddit to look for codes. Nothing about you is sent to them beyond a normal page request, and no cookies are sent.
